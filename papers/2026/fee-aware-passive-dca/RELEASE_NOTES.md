# Fee-Aware Passive DCA — working paper v1.0

**Tao Tao, Independent Researcher** · NR-2026-001 · Manuscript dated 9 October 2026

[Read the full study](https://nomantrade.com/research/can-limit-orders-improve-dca/) · [Publication directory](https://github.com/MagicTaoT/noman-research/tree/fee-aware-passive-dca-v1.0/papers/2026/fee-aware-passive-dca)

The first paper in Noman Research studies a passive-first execution layer for
an already chosen DCA schedule, using 5,837,137 Coinbase BTC-USD one-minute candles.

Under the primary 15/30 bps maker/taker scenario, the adaptive five-level policy
produced a 9.5065 bps cash-weighted quantity edge: +10.3775 bps from fees and
−0.8710 bps from the price path. The fixed one-level reference did better at
12.8327 bps. The paper also reports exploratory fee scenarios, falling-market
panels, wealth accounting and retail-scale order feasibility.

guaranteed benefits. Fees are fixed scenarios, not current-account tier claims.

live trading code, account journals or production configuration. This release
