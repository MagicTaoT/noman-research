# Corrections and research discussion

Please open an issue identifying the paper ID, version, section or table,
the specific concern, and the supporting reasoning or public evidence.
Distinguish a transcription error from a disagreement about model assumptions.

Do not upload market-data dumps, account statements, credentials, private logs,
or copyrighted full texts. Use links to lawful public sources where appropriate.
Do not include personal or sensitive information in a public issue.

Before proposing a change, read the paper's evidence limits. Candle-model
outcomes must not be relabeled as live fills, guaranteed returns, or current
fee-tier promises. Changes to reported results require a new paper version
and an explanation in its changelog; do not silently replace released findings.

New papers follow [the publication guide](docs/PUBLISHING.md). A maintainer must
confirm author approval, publication scope, rights, disclosures and evidence
status before merging. Run `python3 tools/validate.py` before submission.
